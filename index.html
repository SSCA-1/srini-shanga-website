About Srini & Shanga

About the firm.

Srini & Shanga is a firm of Chartered Accountants established in 2026 at Salem, Tamil Nadu. The firm provides services in taxation, audit and assurance, accounting, business advisory, and representation before tax authorities and appellate forums.

The firm was founded by Srinivasan M and Shangamesh T, both Chartered Accountants.

Our founders

The founders.

Srinivasan M

Co-Founder, Chartered Accountant

Srinivasan M is a Chartered Accountant and Co-Founder of Srini & Shanga, Chartered Accountants. His principal area of practice is income-tax litigation, including representation before the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal and the Commissioner of Income-tax (Appeals). His work covers the drafting of grounds of appeal, statements of facts and written submissions, and representation in assessment, reassessment and penalty proceedings, including faceless proceedings. He also advises on audit, taxation, regulatory compliance and startup advisory.

Shangamesh T

Co-Founder, Chartered Accountant

Shangamesh T is a Chartered Accountant and Co-Founder of Srini & Shanga, Chartered Accountants. His areas of practice include taxation, accounting, regulatory compliance and business advisory services, including the use of AI-based tools for process automation. He has a background in the textile and manufacturing industry.
