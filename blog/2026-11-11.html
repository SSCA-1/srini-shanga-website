Income Tax Updates

No TDS on dividend between IFSC aircraft-leasing units

CBDT has notified that tax is not to be deducted under section 393(1) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 on dividend paid by one IFSC aircraft-leasing unit to another. The payee must furnish Form No. 1(N), and the payer still reports such payments in the TDS statement. The notification is deemed in force from 1 April 2026.

CBDT Notification No. 141/2026 (S.O. 6016(E)). Why it matters: Relevant for IFSC leasing clients: confirm Form 1(N) is on file and that the payments are reported without deduction.

4 November 2026 | Primary source

New rule and form for merger of registered non-profit organisations

New Rule 189A and Form No. 112A set conditions for merging one registered non-profit into another with the same or similar objects. A provisionally registered entity cannot merge with a fully registered one, and objects cannot be amended for five years. Form 112A is due within one month after the end of the month in which the merger takes effect.

CBDT Notification No. 139/2026 (G.S.R. 961(E)), Income-tax (Eighth Amendment) Rules, 2026. Why it matters: Trusts and societies planning a merger now have a defined filing and a restriction on changing objects.

30 October 2026 (listed on the e-filing portal on 3 November 2026) | Primary source

Case Laws

Bombay High Court quashes reassessment issued on a pre-conversion company PAN

The Court set aside the section 148A(1) show cause notice, the section 148A(3) order and the section 148 notice for AY 2022-23, which had been issued against a company that was converted into an LLP in 2018. It also found the reassessment to be a fishing enquiry with no material showing escapement of income.

Aavkar Trade Consultants LLP v. Income Tax Officer, Ward 24(1)(1), Mumbai, Writ Petition (L) 27936 of 2026, Bombay High Court (Colabawalla and Dubash, JJ.). Why it matters: A useful authority when a reassessment notice is addressed to an entity that no longer exists in that form.

4 November 2026 | Primary source

Bombay High Court: RBI prudential-norm breach alone does not disallow interest

The Revenue's appeal was dismissed. The Court held that the Explanation to section 37(1) cannot be used to disallow interest under section 36(1)(iii) only because of a breach of RBI prudential norms, particularly where RBI imposed no penalty.

Commissioner of Income Tax-LTU v. Tata Motors Ltd (successor to Tata Finance Ltd), Income Tax Appeal 20 of 2020, AY 2003-04, Bombay High Court. Why it matters: Helps in defending interest deductions of NBFCs and lenders where a regulatory lapse is alleged.

4 November 2026 | Primary source

Telangana High Court directs recomputation of interest on seized-cash refund

The writ was allowed. The Department must recompute the balance interest under sections 132B(4) and 244A without overlap and release it within eight weeks. The Court held that interest under section 244A is not barred by the DTVSV Act.

K. Mohanlal v. Deputy Commissioner of Income Tax and others, Writ Petition 25987 of 2025, Telangana High Court (Sam Koshy, ACJ and Nandikonda, J.). Why it matters: Relevant to post-search refund claims and settled disputes where interest is still outstanding.

5 November 2026 | Primary source

Orissa High Court sets aside time-barred finding on an appeal filed in 30 days

The CIT(A) order of 24 August 2026 was quashed because the appeal had been filed within the 30 days allowed under section 249, so the limitation finding could not stand. The matter was sent back to the CIT(A) to decide on merits.

M/s MC Patel and Co v. Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) and another, W.P.(C) 32497 of 2026, Orissa High Court (Panigrahi and Sri Raman, JJ.). Why it matters: Check the limitation computation on any appeal dismissed as late before taking it further.

4 November 2026 | Primary source

Delhi High Court: appeal against a Gurugram assessment lies to the Punjab and Haryana High Court

The Department's appeal was dismissed for want of jurisdiction, applying the Supreme Court's ABC Papers ruling that the High Court is determined by the location of the assessing officer, here at Gurugram. The Department gets the benefit of section 14 of the Limitation Act for the time spent.

Principal Commissioner of Income Tax-7 v. Perfetti Van Melle India Pvt Ltd, ITA 882/2026, AY 2021-22, Delhi High Court (Prasad and Bhatnagar, JJ.). Why it matters: Confirm the correct High Court before filing a section 260A appeal.

International News

IRS publishes 2027 inflation adjustments

The IRS announced inflation adjustments for almost 70 provisions for tax year 2027. The standard deduction for married couples filing jointly rises by $1,000 to $33,200 and $16,600 for single filers, and the estate exclusion is $15,480,000. The figures generally apply to returns filed in 2028.

IRS news release IR-2026-133 (Revenue Procedure 2026-35). Why it matters: Useful for advising US-connected clients and NRIs on thresholds for planning.

5 November 2026 | Primary source

Bank of England raises base rate, so HMRC interest rates will rise

The base rate moved to 4.00% from 3.75%. HMRC late-payment interest is base rate plus 4% and repayment interest is base rate minus 1% with a 0.5% floor. The new rates apply from 16 November 2026 for quarterly instalments and from 24 November 2026 for non-quarterly instalments.

HMRC news item, GOV.UK. Why it matters: Relevant for clients with UK tax exposure or cash-flow planning for UK liabilities.

6 November 2026 | Primary source

Treasury and IRS designate over 6,500 Opportunity Zones

All 6,576 nominated census tracts were designated, 2,594 of them rural, effective 1 January 2027 for ten years. The release states that the incentive was made permanent and that enhanced rural incentives were added.

IRS news release IR-2026-132. Why it matters: A reference point for investors considering US capital gains deferral structures.
